Personal project · Sales analysis

Superstore Sales Analysis — Discount vs Profit

Why did Furniture sell almost as much as Technology but earn a 2.49% margin (Technology: 17.4%)? I split 9,994 orders (2014–2017) by discount level in SQL and found where the profit disappears.

  • Average profit per order: +66.9 with no discount, +26.5 up to 20%, −97.18 above 20%.
  • 1,393 orders with discounts above 20% lost 135,376 in total.
  • Tables and Bookcases were the loss-makers; Central gave big discounts most often (27.68% of orders) and had the lowest profit.
Transactions9,994
Profit margin12.5%
Loss, discount >20%−135,376

Correlation, not proof of cause: heavily discounted products may already have thin margins. Data is the public Kaggle Superstore sample.
